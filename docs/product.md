# Product Overview (Canonical Summary)

**AI Form Assistant** is a multilingual document-understanding and guided form-completion platform that helps individuals and organizations understand, answer, validate, and export official forms written in unfamiliar languages.

## Core Product Rules

1. **Three Explicit Language Dimensions**:
   - `sourceLanguage`: Language of the original uploaded or selected form.
   - `userLanguage`: Language used for questions, plain-language explanations, and clarifications (`LTR` and `RTL` supported).
   - `outputLanguage`: Language rendered into the final completed PDF.
2. **Strict Non-Legal Assistant Boundary**:
   - The product explains form terminology in plain words, normalizes and validates answers, and fills PDF fields. It never provides legal, immigration, tax, medical, or financial advice, never determines eligibility, and never invents missing user data.
3. **Human-in-the-Loop Confirmation**:
   - Original raw answers are always preserved alongside normalized canonical values and target-language output values. Users review and explicitly confirm all answers before final PDF export.
